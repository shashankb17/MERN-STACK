Danielle de Villiers

Danielle de Villiers is the wife of former South African cricketer, AB de Villiers. She is one of the most well-known faces in the wives and girlfriends community of cricketers. Her Instagram bio states that she is an entrepreneur. She is the Co-owner and creator of The FUN ROOM, Gary Rom Hair Salon, and Soma Laser & Skincare Clinic, Pretoria.
